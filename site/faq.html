Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers for orders and product support

Find quick answers about products, orders, shipping, returns, and customer support.

1. WHAT IS ABL PHARMA?

ABL Pharma provides performance-support supplements for bodybuilders, fitness enthusiasts, and individuals who are new to supplementation. Our formulas focus on ingredient quality, appropriate dosages, and support for a variety of fitness goals.

2. HOW DO I CHOOSE THE RIGHT PRODUCT?

Review each product page to learn about the product’s intended purpose, ingredients, serving instructions, and warnings. Choose the formula that best matches your goals. For additional product information, contact us at sales@ablpharma.com.

3. HOW SHOULD I TAKE ABL PHARMA PRODUCTS?

Always follow the serving instructions and warnings printed on the product label. Do not exceed the recommended serving. Read the complete label before using any product.

4. CAN I USE MULTIPLE ABL PHARMA PRODUCTS TOGETHER?

Some customers may choose to use more than one supplement. However, compatibility depends on the ingredients, dosages, medications, health conditions, and individual circumstances. Review each product label and consult a qualified healthcare professional before combining supplements.

5. HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO NOTICE RESULTS?

Results vary depending on the product, consistency of use, diet, exercise, lifestyle, and the individual. ABL Pharma does not guarantee a specific result or timeline.

6. ARE ABL PHARMA PRODUCTS SAFE?

ABL Pharma products should only be used according to their labels. Supplements may affect individuals differently and may interact with medications or medical conditions. Consult a healthcare professional before use, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or managing a health condition.

7. WHERE CAN I FIND THE INGREDIENTS AND SERVING INFORMATION?

Ingredient details, serving instructions, capsule counts, warnings, and other product information are listed on each product page and on the physical product label.

8. HOW QUICKLY ARE ORDERS PROCESSED?

Orders are generally processed within 4 business days, excluding weekends and holidays. Orders placed after 3:00 PM will begin processing on the following business day. A shipping notification will be sent when the order ships.

9. WHAT IS THE RETURN POLICY?

Unopened and unused products may be eligible for return when the request is submitted within 10 days of delivery. Proof of purchase is required. Opened products, partially used products, clearance or sale items, gift cards, promotional items, and products purchased from third-party retailers are non-refundable.

For complete details, review the Policies page.
